# Algorithms and Models

These pages describe the best practice for achieving understandable, winning AI and delivering it the form of a Twelve Analyst. 

![Screenshot 2025-07-31 at 10.00.57.png](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Screenshot_2025-07-31_at_10.00.57.png)

Our models follow these principles:

1, They are explainable in football terms. (”**understandable”**)

2, They should be relate to measurements of goals scored and conceded. (”**help teams win”**)

3, We use the best motivated approach to each problem we solve (**”advanced AI”**) 

## The basics

The first thing to do is read our [modelling philosophy](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Modelling%20Philosophy%20237fdb4493c5807295d7f0ef0fd2b5de.md) to understand how we approach creating models that fulfils these criteria. The main activity at Twelve rests in [building Analysts](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Building%20a%20Twelve%20Analyst%2022cfdb4493c58092a87cc234093aabb3.md) (AI agents) which talk about players, matches and seasons. These analysts are primarily built around the idea of breacking the game into [qualities, metrics and summaries](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Qualities,%20Metrics%20and%20Summaries%20177fdb4493c580a3ba2ac1e37340d7ce.md). These are then used in [wordalisations](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Wordalisations%20238fdb4493c58045a4fdd4ba6bcca6b0.md), which convert qualities and summaries into texts and [visualisations](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Visualisations%20229fdb4493c580dda07ad47f5c76e71d.md), which show the data. 

## The models

The most important models used in our basic functionality is z-values. We also build more advanced models. In particular our [transfer model](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Transfer%20model%20225fdb4493c580108f53f1e390d473df.md) allows us to predict movement between leagues. Our [attack and defence](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Team%20strength%20model%20225fdb4493c5803b90b0d2f21281e3d1.md) ratings compare team performances. A major part of our current work is building a [Total Football](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Total%20Football%20225fdb4493c58003931af465afedfe11.md) model, based on posession chains, a novel approach to expected threat and then linked back to team ratings. The [financial model](Algorithms%20and%20Models/Financial%20model%20225fdb4493c58032858acd1f01c9ba97.md) looks at how qualities of a player affect their value in the future and how much they will contribute to success on the pitch. We are extending on this model during autumn 2025. The idea of this model  will be to give a fully integrated picture of what a club needs to do to improve within its budget.
