How to use Ask Ashbourne

1. What it can help with

Subject choices, exams, absence and leave, joining the school, school rules, opening hours, who to contact, and support.

2. What it can't do

It can't see records, do homework, submit, book or contact anyone for you, or give medical advice.

3. Example questions

  • How do I pick my GCSE options?
  • How do I get extra time in my exams?
  • Can we take a family holiday in term time?
  • We're moving. How do we transfer mid-year?
  • Can I use my phone at lunch?
  • How do I reset my school Microsoft 365 password?

4. If you need help now

Talk to your tutor, your Head of Year or the Designated Safeguarding Lead (name and how to find them not confirmed yet). Call Childline free on 0800 1111. In an emergency, call 999.

Telling Ask Ashbourne something does not tell a member of staff; please talk to someone directly. (This wording is waiting for the DSL's decision on logging.)
