## AF3: facets (draft, review inline)
A facet is one answerable sub-question. Each facet is tagged with the stage(s) it serves. This is a coverage map, not a sequence. The generator drafted the answers with these rules:
- Every date, number or school-specific figure is a [TBD] placeholder. Nothing is invented.
- Each answer responds to the user's situation, not just their wording.
- Answers say plainly what the bot can't do (submit, book, contact).

⚠ = THIN: a stage backed by only one or two facets. The fix is to write more facets, not to merge the stage. Suggest extra facets for these if you can.
